## Register the business 

**Choose structure:** simplest and cheapest -> Sole Trader, which is personally liable for debts.

**Get an ABN:** Australian Business Register, can do ABN + business name together

**Register a Business Name:** with ASIC if not trading with personal name. ($45 per year, $104 for 3 years).

Search trademarks (IP Australia) and social/domain names. Availability on ASIC.

**GST:** register once turnover hits $75,000

**Business Bank Account:** with basic accounting

## Key Legal and Product Requirements

- **Australian Consumer Law (ACL):** Accurate descriptions, fair pricing, and consumer guarantees (goods must be of acceptable quality and fit for purpose). Having clear returns policy -> faulty items generally need a remedy -> change-of-mind returns are optional but good for customer trust.

- **Care Labelling:** Mandatory for new clothing under the Consumer Goods Information Standard 2023. Labels must be permanent and in Eng and approved care symbols (ISO 3758).

- **Insurance** public liability, product liability at min 

- **Contracts:** supplier/manufacturer agreements, website terms and privacy policy

## Practical Launch Sequence 

1. Pick a niche (style, casual, streetwear etc.)

2. Source samples and work out pricing/margins 

3. Reg ABN 

4. Set up sales channel (website + Ins) + photos/descriptions 

5. Get basic insurance 

6. Soft-launch and iterate
