# Three Coins Differences

**Date started:** 2026-11-5
**Tags:** `Crypto`, `Bitcoin`, `Etherem` `Solana`, `On-chain Finance`

## Key points
The technical differences of BTC, ETH and Sol in five layers: **Consensus**, **Data Model**, **Execution**, **Programming Language** and **Scaling**

### Consensus (How the network agrees)
- **BTC** uses Proof of Work. Miners race to solve a SHA-256 puzzle, and the winner adds a block about every 1- mins. A transaction is considered safe after about 6 blocks ~an hour  

- **ETH** uses Proof of Stake (Gasper). Validator stake 32 ETH each, and a block is made every 12 seconds. Blocks are grouped into "epochs", and a block is final after 13-15 mins.

- **Sol** uses Proof of Stake with Tower BFT + Proof of History. The leader rotation is known in advance, a slot is 0.4 seconds, and a transaction is effectively final in a few seconds. A faster finality upgrade called Alpenglow aims to cut that to well under a second.
